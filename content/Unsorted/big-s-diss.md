title: Big S's Diss Track
---

On July 18, 2025, [Big S](sam-webster) released a diss track against a selection of GLCL competitors. This sudden reemergence of the Big S persona, who was last seen two years prior, preceded his participation and eventual victory in the [2025 Valorant Free For All](valorant-2025).

# Background and Composition

Big S is a persona of GLCL competitor Sam Webster. Before releasing the diss track, he had last appeared on May 19, 2023, in a promotional video for the [Brother v Brother II](brother-v-brother-ii) matchup between sibling rivals [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) and [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski). Big S is credited as the composer, producer, and lyricist of the diss track.
