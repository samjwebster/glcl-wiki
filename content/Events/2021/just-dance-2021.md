The 2021 Just Dance Greatest Hits Invitational was the planned third annual Just Dance tournament. It was scheduled to take place on December 23, 2021, but was canceled due to concerns over the continuing COVID-19 pandemic, particularly the rise of the Omicron variant.

# Background

The GLCL returned after an almost two-year-long hiatus due to COVID-19. The [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2021) on November 27 was the first tournament since the [Just Dance 4 Invitational](just-dance-2019) in December 2019. At the event, [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone), [James Elliott](james-elliott) & [Septilence](septilence), Elliott, and [Bri Reilly](bri-reilly) won the [Intercontinental](intercontinental), [Tag Team](tag-team), [Universal](universal), and [UFC](ufc) titles, respectively.

The GLCL was already set to go on a winter hiatus until May 2022, but the hiatus came early after Just Dance was canceled. The GLCL returned in June with the [Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2022).
