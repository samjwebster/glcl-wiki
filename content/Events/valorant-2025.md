The 2025 Valorant Free For All was a three-way match for the [Intercontinental Title](intercontinental) between defending champion [Septilence](septilence), rival [James Elliott](james-elliott), and wildcard [Big S](sam-webster). After the close and thrilling competition, Big S walked away the Intercontinental title. The event was held virtually on August 9, 2025 and streamed live on Twitch.

# Lead Up

The three-way match was publicly announced on August 2, 2025. On that day, all three competitors released video statements describing their investment to the match. 

[Septilence](septilence) expressed frustration having to defend barely one month after his 'match-of-the-decade' title acquisition in Overwatch 2 against James. Despite this, he remained committed to defending his new title and dismissed the skills of his competitors. [James](james-elliott) strongly criticized Septilence's involvement with [Big British Billy](billy-wager) following the attack on Billy at the [2025 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2025), promising that they will "make what happened to Billy happen to you." [Big S](sam-webster), recording inside of a shower, reiterates his desire to collect more titles following his recent [Universal](universal) win in Flip 7 at the [2025 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2025). He mocks his competitors' lack of swag and charisma.

The trailer for the match was released on August 7, 2025, set to "Teardrops" by Bring Me The Horizon.

# Match

The Valorant match consisted of three 9 minute rounds on the map Ascent, with the winner decided by who has the most eliminations at the end of the round. Each round decided the weapon class available: round 1 was shotguns, round 2 was snipers, and round 3 was assault rifles and submachine guns.

## Round 1

Septilence drew first blood on James at A site. As the early round developed, Big S was ahead by 7 eliminations, but a thrilling two-minute elimination spree by Septilence reduced his lead to 1. With three minutes remaining, Septilence took the lead. James too surpassed Big S with less than two minutes on the clock, but it was not enough to close the gap to Septilence. Septilence won Round 1 with 24 eliminations, followed by James and Big S with 19 and 18 eliminations respectively.

## Round 2

Septilence continued his Round 1 intensity by quickly eliminating James and Big S to start the round. Less than three minutes later, Septilence led by 7 eliminations, followed by Big S. Big S rallied by controlling the map's middle, bringing the score to a tie. James could not hit an early stride, finding only 2 eliminations by halfway through the round. The match remained neck-and-neck until Big S was able to gain a decent lead with not enough time for Septilence to respond. Big S won Round 2 with 29 eliminations, followed by Septilence and James with 27 and 6 eliminations respectively.

## Round 3

Big S opened the match by eliminating Septilence. The early round was dominated by Septilence who gained an early 4-elimination lead ahead of Big S and James. With more than five minutes remaining, Big S took the lead with Septilence and James both very close behind. For almost three minutes, Septilence tried to close the gap while Big S barely maintained it. With barely two minutes remaining, Big S managed a 5-elimination lead over Septilence, which grew to 9 as the timer ran down. Big S won Round 3 and secured the Intercontinental victory with 34 eliminations, followed by Septilence and James with 25 and 17 eliminations respectively.

# Aftermath

In an interview with Chad Bradley following Big S's Intercontinental victory, Big S confirms his intent to gain the Tag Team belt and become the next [Triple Champion](tags/triple-champion).
