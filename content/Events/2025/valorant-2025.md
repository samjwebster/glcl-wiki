The 2025 Valorant Free For All was a three-way match for the [Intercontinental Title](intercontinental) between defending champion [Septilence](septilence), rival [James Elliott](james-elliott), and wildcard [Big S](sam-webster). Following a closely contested match, Big S captured the Intercontinental title. The event was held virtually on August 9, 2025, and streamed live on Twitch.

# Lead Up

The three-way match was publicly announced on August 2, 2025. On the day of the announcement, all three competitors released video statements outlining their stakes in the upcoming match.

[Septilence](septilence) expressed frustration regarding his obligation to defend the title barely one month after his "match-of-the-decade" victory in Overwatch 2 against James. Despite his grievances, he remained committed to defending his new title and dismissed the capabilities of his competitors. [James](james-elliott) strongly criticized Septilence's alignment with [Big British Billy](billy-wager) following the attack on Billy at the [2025 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2025), promising that they would "make what happened to Billy happen to you." [Big S](sam-webster), recording his statement from inside a shower, reiterated his desire to collect additional titles following his recent [Universal](universal) win in Flip 7 at the [2025 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2025). He further mocked his competitors' percieved lack of "swag" and charisma.

A promotional trailer for the match was released on August 7, 2025, featuring the song "Teardrops" by Bring Me The Horizon.

# Match

The Valorant match consisted of three 9-minute rounds on the map Ascent, with the round winner determined by highest eliminations at the end of each round. Each round featured a restricted weapon class: round one utilized shotguns, round two featured snipers, and round three permitted assault rifles and submachine guns.

## Round 1

Septilence secured first blood on James at A site. As the early round developed, Big S established a seven-elimination lead, but a rapid two-minute elimination spree by Septilence reduced the deficit to one. With three minutes remaining, Septilence took the lead. James too surpassed Big S with less than two minutes remaining on the clock, though it was insufficient to close the gap on the leader. Septilence won Round 1 with 24 eliminations, followed by James with 19 and Big S with 18.

## Round 2

Septilence maintained his momentum into the second round, quickly eliminating both James and Big S in the opening seconds. Less than three minutes later, Septilence held a seven-elimination lead over Big S. However, Big S rallied by controlling the map's middle section, eventually forcing a tie score. James struggled to establish an early rhythm, securing only two eliminations by the midway point of the round. The match remained highly competitive until Big S established a definitive lead late in the round. Big S won Round 2 with 29 eliminations, followed by Septilence with 27 and James with six.

## Round 3

Big S opened the final round by eliminating Septilence. The early phase of the round was dominated by Septilence, who gained a quick four-elimination lead over Big S and James. With over five minutes remaining, Big S overtook the lead, with Septilence and James remaining in close contention. For the next three minutes, Septilence attempted to close the deficit while Big S successfully defended his margin. With two minutes remaining, Big S extended his lead to five eliminations, which widened to nine as time expired. Big S won Round 3 to secure the Intercontinental victory with 34 eliminations, followed by Septilence with 25 and James with 17.

# Aftermath

In a post-match interview with Chad Bradley following his Intercontinental victory, Big S confirmed his intentions to pursue the Tag Team title and become the GLCL's next [Triple Champion](tags/triple-champion).
