Silicon Valley are a duo allianceship between [Sam Webster](sam-webster) and [Matt Berlin](matt-berlin). They competed for the [Tag Team](tag-team) title at the [2022 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2022) but were ultimately defeated by defending co-champions [Septilence](septilence) and [James Elliott](james-elliott).

## Namesake

The Silicon Valley region, roughly covering the southern San Francisco Bay Area, California, is a global hub for the American tech industry. Both Sam and Matt majored in computer science at their respective universities.
