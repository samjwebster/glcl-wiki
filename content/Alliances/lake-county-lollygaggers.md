The Lake County Lollygaggers are a Rocket League trio consisting of members [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken), [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski), and [Sam Webster](sam-webster). In their only competitive appearance at the [2022 Septilence Presents: Carball Contenders Series](carball-contenders-2022), the Lollygaggers' skills earned them the Tag Team Title.
