The Hoodies are a duo allianceship between [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) and [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken). Initially forming for the [2019 Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2019), they would go on to win the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles at the event. They disbanded shortly after a disappointing first-round loss in the [2021 Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-world-cup).
