Fintan Bracken is a veteran competitor and decorated champion in the GLCL. Making his debut at the tail end of 2017, he quickly transformed from a struggling rookie into a dominant main-event force. Fintan is best known for his record-setting achievements, including becoming the league's third-ever [Triple Crown Champion](tags/triple-crown) and establishing the fastest timeline to complete the feat. Across his career, he held the [Intercontinental](intercontinental), [Universal](universal), and [Tag Team](tag-team) titles, representing winning alliances like [The Hoodies](hoodies) and the [Lake County Lollygaggers](lake-county-lollygaggers).

# Competitive History

## Intercontinental Championship reigns (2017–2018)

Fintan made his first appearance at the [Super Smash Bros. Brawl Classic](super-smash-bros-2017) on December 30, 2017. Despite a solid performance, Fintan was defeated by [James Elliott](james-elliott) in the first round.

On January 13, Fintan entered the [WWE 2k18 Spice Cube Invitational](wwe-2018), teaming with [James](james-elliot) in the [Tag Team](tag-team) tournament. The two lost to [Septilence](septilence) & [Jason Abbott](jason-abbott). Fintan later lost to [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) in the first round of the singles tournament.

Fintan turned his luck around in the fall of 2018. On August 17, he beat [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) in the first round of a Pac-Man tournament to advance to the semifinals. On September 2, Fintan defeated [Hannah Syrus](hannah-syrus) to advance to the finals. He immediately faced [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) in the finals for the vacant [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship. Fintan won the match to become Intercontinental Champion for the first time, becoming the first person of immigrant stock to win the title.

[Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) was far from done with Fintan. He challenged the new champion to a rematch in Super Mario Strikers on September 15. In a vicious and heart-stopping slugfest, Anthony defeated Fintan. A final rubber match was scheduled for September 29 in Frogger. The two arch-rivals faced off one last time in a neck-and-neck battle. At the end of the night, Anthony’s hand was raised high to retain the title and win the feud. 

## Tag Team Championship reigns (2018–2019)

Following his back-to-back losses to [Anthony](anthony-iacovone), Fintan moved to the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship scene. He teamed up with [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) to beat [Alexis Cardinale](alexis-cardinale) & [Hannah Syrus](hannah-syrus) in New Super Mario Bros. Wii for the Tag Team titles on October 13. He and Braden defended the titles on November 1 against [Spice Cube](devan-welch) and a returning [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski). 

On November 24, at the [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2018), [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) & Fintan fought their way through the tag team tournament. In the finals, the Tag Team Champions defeated [Spice Cube](devan-welch) & [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) in a brutal match to retain the titles and become the first Tag Team champs to walk in and out of a tournament with the titles. Fintan fought through the first two rounds of the singles tournament to make it to the finals. In the final battle, Fintan duked it out with Connor, Spice, and [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone). Connor eventually won the match and the title.

The next day, Fintan rebounded with a win in Poker against [Universal](universal) Champion [Jason Abbott](jason-abbott), [Matt Berlin](matt-berlin), [Anthony](anthony-iacovone), and [Spice Cube](spice-cube). By winning the Universal Championship, Fintan became the third [Triple Crown Champion](tags/triple-crown), and the fastest individual to complete the achievement.

Fintan’s first Universal title defense was against [Spice Cube](devan-welch) in Connect Four on December 14. He successfully defended the title again on December 27, in Risk: Game of Thrones against [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) and [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall). On December 28, Fintan defended the Universal title in an Air Hockey tournament. He was eliminated in the first round by [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski), who went on to win the tournament and the title.

On January 12, 2019, Fintan defended the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles with [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) at the [WWE 2k19 Spice Cube Invitational](wwe-2019). In a shocking upset, the champions lost to [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) & [Charlie Nunes](charlie-nunes). As a result, Braden & Fintan were eliminated and their titles were vacated. In the singles tournament, Fintan beat Charlie Nunes in the first round but was destroyed by [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) in the second.

On March 8, Fintan won the [Universal](universal) title for the second time, defeating [Jenna](jenna-rosciszewski) in a game of Checkers. On April 18, Fintan defended the title in Stratego in a winning effort against [Charlie Nunes](charlie-nunes). On May 19, Fintan retained the belt in Dark Tower, defeating Charlie Nunes, [Alexis Cardinale](alexis-cardinale), and a returning [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar). On June 8, Fintan defended the title for the first time outside of Ohio, beating [Sam Webster](sam-webster) in Checkers in San Antonio, Texas.

On June 22, Fintan lost to [Sam](sam-webster) in the first round of the [Mario Super Sluggers World Series](mario-super-sluggers-2019). That same day, he lost the [Universal](universal) Championship to [Charlie Nunes](charlie-nunes) in an UNO 6-pack match also involving [Spice Cube](devan-welch), [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski), [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall), and [Sam Webster](sam-webster). Fintan’s second reign ended at 106 days.

On October 12, Fintan defeated [Spice Cube](devan-welch), [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall), and [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski) to win the vacant [Universal](universal) Championship for a third time.

At the [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2019) on November 30, Fintan lost the title after getting eliminated by a returning [James Elliott](james-elliott) in the first round of a 4-man Guess Who? tournament. Later that night, Fintan formed an unlikely partnership with his former nemesis, [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone). Christening themselves [The Hoodies](hoodies), the two dominated the competition. The team tore through [Charlie Marthaler](charlie-marthaler) & [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar), [Matty B](matt-berlin) & [Dan Ruhe](dan-ruhe), and [James Elliott](james-elliott) & [Septilence](septilence) to win the vacant [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship. In the singles tournament, Fintan advanced to the finals to face [Connor](connor-rosciszewski), Spice, and [Young Jon](jon-shields). In the climax of the main event, Jon suicide attacked Spice to cost him the game and indirectly hand Connor the title. Spice began to complain and request a restart of the match, but Fintan accused Spice of being a coward the entire tournament. Connor ended the conflict by denying both men a rematch. 

On December 21, [The Twins](twins) challenged [The Hoodies](hoodies) for the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship. Despite an admirable performance, the challengers failed to reclaim the titles. Fintan gave the challengers props in a post-match interview, but asserted that The Hoodies would be champions for a long time.

## Hiatus (2020–2021)
The [Intercontinental](intercontinental), [Tag Team](tag-team), and [Universal](universal) Championships were put on hiatus after the [2019 Just Dance 4 Invitational](just-dance-2019), with the [Guitar Hero World Tour](guitar-hero-2020) scheduled for May 23, 2020. However, due to the COVID-19 pandemic, the event was cancelled and the hiatus was extended until further notice. [The Hoodies](hoodies) were scheduled to face [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) & [Spice Cube](devan-welch) in Rock Band at the Guitar Hero World Tour event.

## Return and the Lake County Lollygaggers (2021–2023)
After almost two years of inactivity, the third annual [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2021) was announced on September 3, 2021. Fintan returned at the event on November 27. He and [Anthony](anthony-iacovone) were eliminated in the first round of the [tag team](tag-team) tournament. He made it to the finals of the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) tournament, but was defeated by Anthony himself. [The Hoodies](hoodies) split up as a tag team shortly thereafter. 

On September 30, 2022, Fintan defeated [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar) in the opening round of [Septilence Presents: The GLCL Carball Contender’s Series](carball-contenders-2022). In a brutal loss, [Sam](sam-webster) crushed Fintan 2-0 to eliminate The Revolutionary from the tournament. On December 18, he formed the [Lake County Lollygaggers](lake-county-lollygaggers) with [Sam Webster](sam-webster) and [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski). The trio earned the right to challenge GLCL [Tag Team](tag-team) Champions [James Elliott](james-elliott) & [Septilence](septilence) in Rocket League. Fintan & Sam represented the Lollygaggers to successfully claim the titles.

On December 22, Fintan was eliminated in the second round of the [Ultimate Chicken Horse Smackdown](ultimate-chicken-horse-2022). The [Lake County Lollygaggers](lake-county-lollygaggers), represented by [Sam](sam-webster) & [Braden](braden-rosciszewski), lost the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championships that night. The Lollygaggers have not appeared in tag team competition since.

On June 17, 2023, Fintan interrupted his former ally and GLCL Intercontinental Champion [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski), launched a verbal assault on him, then challenged him to a match at the [Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2023) on July 7. However, he later pulled out of the event and was replaced by [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski).
