Ryan Yanchar is a founding member and veteran competitor of the GLCL. He made his debut in the first-ever GLCL competition, which he won to become the inaugural GLCL [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Champion. A highly decorated competitor, Ryan has won the Intercontinental Championship twice, the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship thrice, and the [Universal](universal) Championship twice, making him the first of only two competitors to ever [hold all three titles simultaneously](tags/triple-champion). He is one of the original main-eventers in the GLCL, establishing his position with high-profile wins, feuds, and storylines against [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski), [Bri Reilly](bri-reilly), and [Septilence](septilence). Following the COVID-19 pandemic, Ryan’s appearances in the GLCL became significantly less frequent. His legacy remains cemented as one of the most popular and decorated competitors of the GLCL’s foundational years.

# Competitive History

## Inaugural Intercontinental Champion (2017)
On January 14, 2017, Ryan debuted in the first match in the history of the [Intercontinental Championship](intercontinental). The match served as Game 1 of the [Mario Party 8 Best-of-Six](mario-party-2017) series. Ryan won the first three games of the series to become the favorite to win the title. After [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) won Game 4, Game 5 stood to determine the inaugural champion if Ryan secured a victory. Had Braden won Games 5 and 6, the series would have tied, sending the title decision to a select number of minigames. Ultimately, [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski) won Game 5, and Ryan was officially awarded the Intercontinental Championship.

Ryan lost to [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) on April 13 in the first round of the [Mario Super Sluggers World Series](mario-super-sluggers-2017), which forced him to vacate the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) title. Ryan later entered the first annual [Mario Kart Wii Grand Prix](mario-kart-2017) on June 15, but was eliminated in the second round by [Alex](alex-ruhe) and [Dan Ruhe](dan-ruhe).

## Championship pursuits (2017–2018)

[Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski) issued a challenge to Ryan, [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski), and [James Elliott](james-elliott) in Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare. Ryan accepted the challenge, but on September 16, James swept the field to win the Intercontinental Championship in dominant fashion.

A Twitter poll was launched soon after the Modern Warfare four-way match to determine the number-one contender for the Intercontinental title. Ryan edged out [Septilence](septilence), [Dan Ruhe](dan-ruhe), and [Charlie Nykiel](charlie-nykiel) to earn the title shot. [James](james-elliott) and Ryan agreed to compete in EA Sports UFC 2 on September 28, where Ryan lost decisively after five rounds. On October 13, James and Ryan reluctantly formed a tag team to face [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski) and Septilence in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 3 for the inaugural [Tag Team](tag-team) Championships, but failed to capture the titles.

On December 22, Ryan teamed with [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) to take on Connor and Septilence in Madden NFL 17. The challengers won the match to capture their first [Tag Team](tag-team) Championships. Ryan then entered the Super Smash Bros. Brawl Classic on December 30, defeating [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski) in the first round before losing to Intercontinental Champion Bri Reilly in the second round.

Ryan competed in the first-ever match for the [Universal](universal) Championship in Monopoly on January 3, 2018. He lost to [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski) in a five-way match that also involved [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) and [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski). Two days later, Ryan defeated Jenna, Braden, Connor, and his father, [Harry](harry-yanchar), in Risk to win the Universal Championship. With this victory, he became the first double champion and the first [Triple Crown Champion](tags/triple-crown) in league history. Later that same day, Ryan and Braden lost the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles to Jenna and [Scott Rosciszewski](scott-rosciszewski) in a game of darts.

On January 13, Ryan teamed with his longtime rival [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) for the [WWE 2K18 Spice Cube Invitational](wwe-2018) Tag Team tournament. Connor & Ryan defeated [Septilence](septilence) & [Jason Abbott](jason-abbott) in a Steel Cage match in the finals to win the vacant [Tag Team](tag-team) Championships, making both men the first two-time Tag Team Champions. In the concurrent singles tournament, Ryan attempted to reclaim the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship. He faced Septilence in the first round; following multiple instances of AI interference and RNG glitches, the match was restarted several times. Septilence eventually defeated Ryan, signaling the beginning of an extended rivalry. Ryan lost the [Universal](universal) Championship the following day in a Scrabble four-way match involving Connor and James.

On February 16, [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) challenged Ryan, [Braden](braden-rosciszewski), and [James](james-elliott) to a rematch in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2. Despite an impressive showing from Ryan, James secured the victory to retain the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship. Ryan avenged his previous Super Smash Bros. Brawl loss to [Bri Reilly](bri-reilly) on March 31 during the first round of the [Mario Super Sluggers World Series](mario-super-sluggers-2018). However, Bri quickly retaliated later that day by teaming with Septilence to defeat Ryan and Connor for the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles.

On April 7, Ryan eliminated [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) from the [Mario Super Sluggers World Series](mario-super-sluggers-2018), effectively disbanding their tag team. The same day, Ryan faced [Septilence](septilence) in the tournament finals for the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship. Septilence defeated Ryan, further escalating their feud. On May 19, the final four competitors of the Mario Super Sluggers World Series (Ryan, Connor, [James](james-elliott), and Septilence) attempted to settle their score in a Gang Beasts four-way match for the Intercontinental title. The competitors battled for 25 consecutive rounds, resulting in a three-way, seven-win tie between James, Ryan, and Septilence. Ryan was eliminated after falling from a hanging shipping container, and James won the final round to reclaim the title. Following this loss, Ryan vowed to win the upcoming [Mario Kart Wii Grand Prix](mario-kart-2018) on June 15.

Prior to the Grand Prix, Ryan teamed with [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski) on June 13 to win the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles in a Mario Party 8 match against [Septilence](septilence) and [James Elliott](james-elliott) (who was substituting for [Bri Reilly](bri-reilly)). In the opening match of the [Mario Kart Wii Grand Prix](mario-kart-2018), Ryan faced [Universal](universal) Champion James Elliott, [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski), and [Spice Cube](devan-welch) in Monopoly Gamer: Mario Kart. Ryan won the match to reclaim the Universal title. He later teamed with [Jenna](jenna-rosciszewski) to successfully defend the Tag Team titles against [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) and [Kourtney Syrus](kourtney-syrus) in Mario Kart Wii.

Entering the singles tournament, Ryan defeated [Mark Hermann](mark-hermann), [Bri Reilly](bri-reilly), and [Jason Abbott](jason-abbott) to advance to the finals. In the championship round, Ryan defeated his arch-nemesis, [Septilence](septilence), to capture the vacant [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship, winning the title for the first time in over a year. On June 20, Ryan successfully defended the title against Bri Reilly in Guitar Hero: Metallica.

Eight days later, Ryan and [Jenna](jenna-rosciszewski) defended the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles in a shuffleboard tournament, but were eliminated in the second round, vacating the championships. Later that evening, Ryan lost the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship in a seven-player Quiplash match to [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski). The match also featured James Elliott, Mark Hermann, [Septilence](septilence), [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski), and [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski). On July 3, Ryan faced [Matt Berlin](matt-berlin) in the finals of a Battleship tournament. The match ended in a no-contest, and Ryan relinquished the [Universal](universal) belt due to a scoring error.

## Part-Time status (2018–2019)

Ryan entered the [Super Smash Bros. for Wii U](super-smash-bros-2018) singles tournament on July 6 but was eliminated in the second round; [Mark Hermann](mark-hermann) eventually won the tournament. Seeking to enter an upcoming Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 3 four-way match as the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Champion, Ryan challenged Mark in Call of Duty: Black Ops and Call of Duty: Black Ops II on July 13. Mark defeated Ryan in a close match but chose to vacate the belt so the four-way match could determine the champion. Three days later, in the third and final four-way encounter between Ryan, [James Elliott](james-elliott), [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski), and [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski), James secured another clean sweep. James subsequently retained the title against Ryan and Braden in Minecraft on August 6. This marked Ryan’s final match as a full-time competitor before leaving for college.

Ryan returned for the [Just Dance 3 Invitational](just-dance-2018) on December 22, where he was eliminated in the first round by [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Champion [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski) and [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall). Six days later, he defeated [James Elliott](james-elliott) in the first round of an air hockey tournament before losing to [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski) in the subsequent round. Ryan returned to school shortly thereafter.

On May 19, 2019, Ryan made a surprise return to challenge for the [Universal](universal) Championship, losing to reigning champion [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken) in a four-way match that also included [Charlie Nunes](charlie-nunes) and [Alexis Cardinale](alexis-cardinale). On July 12, at the [Mario Kart Wii Grand Prix](mario-kart-2019), Ryan competed in the second annual Monopoly Gamer: Mario Kart four-way match against Universal Champion [Spice Cube](devan-welch), [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski), and [James Elliott](james-elliott), but failed to win the title. He then teamed with James to earn a [Tag Team](tag-team) title shot by defeating [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) & Spice Cube, though they ultimately lost to the reigning champions, Braden & [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski). In the singles tournament, Ryan advanced to the finals of the first 32-competitor Mario Kart Wii Grand Prix alongside [Kourtney Syrus](kourtney-syrus), [Charlie Nykiel](charlie-nykiel), and the debuting [David Sherman](david-sherman), ultimately losing to Charlie.

Ryan’s final appearance of the summer occurred in a chess tournament for the [Universal](universal) title on August 7, where he defeated [Mike Rellick](mike-rellick) to advance to the finals. In the final round, Ryan faced [Matty B](matt-berlin). Mirroring their July 2018 encounter, the match ended in a no-contest, leaving the title vacant. On November 30, Ryan returned for the [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2019). He teamed with [Charlie Marthaler](charlie-marthaler) in the [Tag Team](tag-team) tournament, where they were defeated by [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken) & [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) of [The Hoodies](hoodies).

## Hiatus (2020–2021)

The [Intercontinental](intercontinental), [Tag Team](tag-team), and [Universal](universal) Championships were placed on hiatus following the [2019 Just Dance 4 Invitational](just-dance-2019), with a [Guitar Hero World Tour](guitar-hero-2020) event scheduled for May 23, 2020. However, due to the COVID-19 pandemic, the event was canceled and the league's hiatus was extended indefinitely.

## Sporadic Returns (2022–Present)

After a two-and-a-half-year absence, Ryan returned to the league at the [2022 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2022), advancing to the second round before elimination. He subsequently entered [Septilence Presents: The GLCL Carball Contender’s Series](carball-contenders-2022), losing to [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken) in the opening round. On November 26, Ryan competed in a Jeopardy! Intercontinental Championship three-way match against [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) and [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski), which was won by Charlie.

Ryan returned for his first match in over three years on December 27, 2025, appearing as the surprise third competitor representing the [Big British Kingdom](big-british-kingdom) at [Boomerang Fu: TURF WARS](boomerang-fu-2025), where his team narrowly lost. On January 8, 2026, [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski) confronted Ryan regarding his unexpected return to the league. Connor defeated Ryan in a Call of Duty: Black Ops Best-of-Three series.
