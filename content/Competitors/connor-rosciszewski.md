Connor Rosciszewski is a founding member of the GLCL. He made his debut in the first-ever GLCL competition. A highly-decorated competitor, Connor has won the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship a record seven times in addition to five [Tag Team](tag-team) and two [Universal](universal) titles. He is best known for his calculating and egomaniacal personas, both in singles competition as well as a part of [The Family Business](family-business), which have propelled him to main-event appearances for much of his career.

# Competitive History

## Rises and falls (2017)

Connor debuted in the first match in GLCL history, on January 14, 2017. This was Game 1 of the [Mario Party 8 Best-of-Six](mario-party-2017) series. Connor lost the first four games, but won Game 5 to cost his brother, [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski), the series and hand [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar) the inaugural [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship.

Connor defeated [Ryan](ryan-yanchar) on April 13, in the first round of the [Mario Super Sluggers World Series](mario-super-sluggers-2017), to vacate the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) title. Connor went on to beat [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) and [James Elliott](james-elliott) to reach the finals of the tournament, where he came up short against [Septilence](septilence).

Connor entered the first annual [2017 Mario Kart Wii Grand Prix](mario-kart-2017) on June 15, but lost in the finals to [Dan Ruhe](dan-ruhe).

## Championship reigns (2017–2019)

On August 19, Connor challenged [Dan Ruhe](dan-ruhe) to an [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship match in WWE 2K14. Despite Dan's best efforts, Connor snatched the title with relative ease. He immediately issued a challenge to [James](james-elliott), [Braden](braden-rosciszewski), and [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar) in Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare. On September 16, James won the Intercontinental Championship in a clean sweep.

On October 13, Connor teamed up with [Septilence](septilence) to take on [James Elliott](james-elliott) & [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar) in a Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 3 Survival match for the inaugural [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship. Connor & Mat prevailed to become the first-ever Tag Team Champions. Connor & Mat were challenged by [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) & Ryan in Madden NFL 17, on December 22. The champions were defeated in an agonizingly long match. 

On January 13, 2018, Connor fought his way through the [WWE 2K18 Spice Cube Invitational](wwe-2018). In the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship tournament, he partnered with [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar). The two defeated [Septilence](septilence) & [Jason Abbott](jason-abbott) in a Steel Cage match to win the Tag Team titles. In a brutal Last Man Standing match, Connor overcame [James Elliott](james-elliott) to become the first-ever two-time [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Champion. However, the animosity between Connor and James only continued to intensify.

Connor successfully defended the [IC](intercontinental) title against [Spice Cube](devan-welch) on February 2, in Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games. [James Elliott](james-elliott) challenged Connor to a rematch in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2. Seeing this as a chance at redemption for his humiliating loss in September 2017, Connor accepted. [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar) and [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) both insisted that they deserved to be in the match as well, leading to the creation of a 4-way match on February 16. Despite a much stronger showing by Connor, Ryan, and Braden, the Intercontinental Championship was reclaimed by James in 7 vicious rounds. Connor won the [Universal](universal) Championship in Cards Against Humanity later that night to become the second-ever Triple Crown Champion.

On March 16, Connor lost the [Universal](universal) title to Braden in a Candy Land 3-way also involving their father, [Scott Rosciszewski](scott-rosciszewski). On March 30, Connor reclaimed the Universal Championship in B.S., defeating Braden, [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski), and [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar). 

On March 31, Connor & Ryan lost the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship to [Bri Reilly](bri-reilly) & [Septilence](septilence) at the [Mario Super Sluggers World Series & Tag Team Classic](mario-super-sluggers-2018). To add insult to injury, Ryan defeated Connor on April 7 in the semifinals of the event's [Intercontinental](intercontinental) track.

On May 19, Connor competed in a Gang Beasts 4-way for the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship, against champion [Septilence](septilence), [James Elliott](james-elliott), and [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar). James won the match, provoking Connor to challenge them to a grudge match on June 1.

Eight days later, [James](james-elliott) further stoked flames in the fire by taking Connor’s Universal title in a Risk six-pack also involving [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar), [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski), [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski), and [Harry Yanchar](harry-yanchar). 

On June 1, Connor and [James](james-elliott) went head-to-head in Halo: Reach with the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship on the line. In the last few seconds of the intense duel, James killed Connor to end the match with a tie. James decided to vacate the title out of a newfound respect for their arch-rival, putting their year-long feud to an end.

At Quiplash Dash on June 28, Connor and his brother [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) joined forces as [The Twins](twins) for the first time to take on [Tag Team](tag-team) Champions [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski) & [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar) in Shuffleboard. The brothers were easily defeated by the defending champions. In the main event, Connor defeated Jenna, Braden, Ryan, [James Elliott](james-elliott), [Septilence](septilence), and [Mark Hermann](mark-hermann) to win Ryan’s [Intercontinental](intercontinental) title.

Connor entered the [Super Smash Bros. for Wii U Classic](super-smash-bros-2018) on July 6. He made it to the second round, where he lost to [James Elliott](james-elliott) 1-on-1 for the first time. The [IC](Intercontinental) title was also vacated as a result of his elimination.

On July 16, Connor faced [James](james-elliott), [Ryan](ryan-yanchar), and [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) in one last 4-way in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 3. Poetically, James won in a clean sweep. Connor went on an extended leave of absence after the loss.

Connor returned on November 1, as [Spice Cube's](devan-welch) surprise partner in a match against [Tag Team](tag-team) Champions [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) & [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken). Connor & Spice Cube put on an impressive showing, but failed to capture the titles.

At the [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2018) on November 24, Connor & [Spice Cube](devan-welch) made it to the finals of the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship tournament. In a heated encounter, the unlikely duo lost again to Braden & Fintan. Connor later fought his way to the finals of the Intercontinental Championship tournament, defeating Fintan, Spice Cube, and [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) to win the [IC](intercontinental) title for the fourth time.

Connor defended his [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship in the [Just Dance 3 Invitational](just-dance-2018) on December 22, beating the likes of [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall), [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar), and [Bri Reilly](bri-reilly) on the road to the finals. In the main event, Connor faced none other than his former arch-rival, [James Elliott](james-elliott). In a frenetic bout, Connor managed to defeat his nemesis. In doing so, he became the first singles champion to walk in and out of a standard tournament with their title.

At the [WWE 2K19 Spice Cube Invitational](wwe-2019) on January 12, 2019, Connor was scheduled to face [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) & [Dan Ruhe](dan-ruhe) alongside his teammate, [Spice Cube](devan-welch). Spice did not appear, leaving Connor out to dry. Charlie & Dan denied any offense thrown their way before tossing Connor through a table to win the match and take the [Tag Team](tag-team) title. Connor next defended the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship against [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) and [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken) to enter the finals of the singles tournament. In the main event, Connor faced off against his brother, [Braden](braden-rosciszewski), in an Iron Man match. Though Connor won the match, Braden called him a coward. The champion agreed to a No-DQ rematch minutes later, defeating his brother again.

Surprised by [Braden’s](braden-rosciszewski) grace in losing the rematch, Connor granted his older brother another opportunity at the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship in Peggle. Billed as “18 Years in the Making”, the [Brother vs Brother](brother-vs-brother) match was scheduled to take place on February 17. During this feud, [The Twins](twins) reluctantly teamed up to take on [Tag Team](tag-team) Champions [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) & [Dan Ruhe](dan-ruhe) at the [Super Smash Bros. Ultimate Super Bowl](super-smash-bros-2019) on February 2. In the final part of a Gauntlet match, the Tag Team Champions capitalized on Braden & Connor’s lack of unity to retain the titles. On February 17, Connor defeated Braden to retain the Intercontinental title and end the feud. 

On March 12, Connor unexpectedly lost the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship to [Matty B](matt-berlin) in Dig Dug, ending his reign at 107 days.

Connor turned on his tag team partner, [Spice Cube](devan-welch), to team up with [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) (who betrayed his own partner, [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken)). Representing [The Twins](twins), Braden defeated [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) in a Madden NFL 19 singles match for the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles on April 20. The Twins held the titles for most of 2019, defending them against [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) & Spice Cube, Charlie Marshall & Spice Cube, and [James Elliott](james-elliott) & [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar).

At the [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2019) on November 30, [The Twins](twins) were eliminated in a massive upset against [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) & [Spice Cube](devan-welch). Later that night, Connor defeated [Fintan](fintan-bracken), Spice Cube, and [Young Jon](jon-shields) after Jon killed Spice Cube with a suicide attack. Connor won the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship for the fifth time as a result.

## Heel turn and hiatus (2020–2021)

On December 21, [The Twins](twins) defeated [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) & [Spice Cube](devan-welch) in Halo 3 for a [Tag Team](tag-team) title opportunity against the winners of the [2019 Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2019), [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) & [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken). Despite an admirable performance, the challengers failed to reclaim the championship. After the match, Connor viciously turned on [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) to instantly disband the team. Connor later elaborated on his actions, claiming his brother was dead weight. He cited his success in the Gang Beasts singles tournament as proof that he was better off working alone. 

On December 27, Connor fought his way to the finals of the [Just Dance 4 Invitational](just-dance-2019), where he lost the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) title to [Bri Reilly](bri-reilly).

The [Intercontinental](intercontinental), [Tag Team](tag-team), and [Universal](universal) Championships were put on hiatus after Just Dance 4, with the [Guitar Hero World Tour](guitar-hero-2020) scheduled for May 23, 2020. However, due to the COVID-19 pandemic, the event was canceled and the hiatus was extended until further notice. Connor planned to begin a gimmick called “The Savior of Columbus” in which he claimed to be a messianic figure. 

After almost two years of inactivity, the third annual [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2021) was announced on September 3, 2021. On November 27, Connor returned alongside the rest of the GLCL. Though he proved he hadn’t lost a step on his way to the finals, it was [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) who scored three wins first to win the vacant [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship. Connor’s undefeated streak in the Gang Beasts World Cup was broken as a result.

Undeterred, Connor promised to win the [Just Dance Greatest Hits Invitational](just-dance-2021) in December. However, due to mounting COVID concerns, the tournament was canceled and the GLCL winter hiatus began early.

## ``#OccupyGLCL'' and feud with Braden (2022–2023)

The summer season began on June 12, 2022 with the [2022 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2022), where Connor failed to advance to the second round. 

Connor would enter the first-ever Rocket League tournament, titled [Septilence Presents: The GLCL Carball Contender’s Series](carball-contenders-2022). Leading up to the match, Connor delivered a scathing promo insulting multiple GLCL competitors before referring to his brother and longtime rival [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) as an “embarrassment to \[their\] family”. On September 17, the brothers battled in a Best 2-of-3 first-round matchup. In a thrilling encounter, Braden won in overtime to finally defeat Connor one-on-one for the first time in the GLCL.

Following his defeat at the hands of [Braden](braden-rosciszewski), Connor began peddling conspiracy theories on social media accusing Braden of cheating, even starting the hashtag ``#BradenFraud''. Connor guest hosted Week 3 of the [Carball Contender’s Series](carball-contenders-2022) on the GLCL’s official Twitch channel, returning in Week 4 as [Septilence’s](septilence) co-caster before hosting again for Week 5. 

In November, Connor staged an ``#OccupyGLCL'' protest in Columbus, Ohio. He led a rally bemoaning the GLCL’s "failure" to stop [Braden’s](braden-rosciszewski) purported fraud, and claimed "the people" wanted to see him as GLCL [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Champion. On December 30, at the [Ultimate Chicken Horse Smackdown](ultimate-chicken-horse-2022), he won the IC title for a sixth time and captured Braden’s [Tag Team](tag-team) titles (alongside [Septilence](septilence)) to add insult to injury.

Connor and [Septilence](septilence) dropped the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles to the [Friends in Fear](friends-in-fear) in March, 2023. Connor would continue to reject challenges issued by [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) for the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship. In April 2023, Braden made one final challenge to Connor, stating he was "done" with his brother. Connor bemusingly accepted the challenge, believing that this may be his last chance to show Braden and the rest of the world that he was the superior twin. At [Brother v Brother II](brother-vs-brother-ii) on May 27, Braden defeated Connor to win the GLCL Intercontinental Championship for the first time.

Connor returned as [Braden’s](braden-rosciszewski) surprise partner in an impromptu [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship match against [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski) and [Big Bad Billy](billy-wager) (standing in for [James Elliott](james-elliott)) on November 23. The reunited [Twins](twins) defeated the Friends in Fear in Nintendo Switch Sports to become 2-time GLCL Tag Team Champions as a duo.

## The Family Business (2024–Present)

[The Twins](twins) aligned themselves with [Big Bad Billy](billy-wager) on March 31, before immediately betraying him and revealing their true allegiance to his “Wisewoman”, [Jenna](jenna-rosciszewski). The Twins beat Big Bad Billy down before Jenna defeated him in Just Dance Greatest Hits to claim his [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship. The three siblings henceforth dubbed themselves [The Family Business](family-business) and vowed to enact violence on everyone in their path.

Connor was eliminated in the second round of the [2024 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2024) on June 22. [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) & [Charlie Nunes](charlie-nunes) were scheduled to challenge Connor & [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) for their GLCL [Tag Team](tag-team) Championships, but [The Twins](twins) revealed that they brutally assaulted Charlie before the event. Without a partner and with his back to the wall, Anthony made an alliance with [Big Bad Billy](billy-wager). The duo shockingly defeated Connor & Braden. Furious that their plot backfired and cost them their titles, the Twins blindsided Billy again with a ferocious attack in the parking lot after the event. 

On July 20, [Anthony](anthony-iacovone) returned the favor by savagely attacking Connor while the latter was taking out the trash. Anthony warned that ``\[he’s\] not gonna stop next time'' after leaving Connor in a pool of his own blood.

On August 24, Connor came in first place overall at the [GLCL Olympics](olympics-2024) to win his record-setting seventh GLCL [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship. [Septilence](septilence) earned the right to challenge Connor for the title by coming in second place. Their match was penned for October, but later cancelled due to scheduling issues.

On November 30, a 4-on-4 elimination match was scheduled to end the feud between [The Family Business](family-buseinss) and [Billy](billy-wager). Billy--now going by Big British Billy after [The Twins’](twins) numerous assaults left him with brain trauma and a new identity--recruited [Anthony](anthony-iacovone), [Septilence](septilence), and [Charlie Nunes](charlie-nunes) to fight alongside him as the [Big British Kingdom](big-british-kingdom). Unfortunately, due to severe weather, both [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) and honorary member [James](james-elliott) (who aligned with The Family Business due to a mutual disdain for Billy) pulled out of the event and the match was cancelled.

Dissatisfied with this non-conclusion and itching for a fight, Connor personally called out [Anthony](anthony-iacovone) in February 2025. After trading barbs in an interview, Anthony broke into Connor’s home and shoved him into a washing machine on February 24. Connor laid out the official challenge for an [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship match in Worms W.M.D. On February 28, Connor defeated Anthony to retain the title. After the match, Anthony ambushed Connor and ran off with the title belt.

Connor emerged in March “asking nicely” for the title back. [Anthony](anthony-iacovone) taunted Connor and refused to return the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) belt. After receiving a cryptic warning from Connor, Anthony found his fiancee, [Jade Rush](jade-rush), being held at knifepoint by a psychotic Connor. After a tense confrontation, Anthony managed to talk down Connor and hand over the belt. Furious, Anthony challenged Connor again, this time in Street Fighter V. On March 15, Connor lost the Intercontinental Championship to Anthony. After the match, Anthony brutalized Connor with a steel chair and left him bloodied on the floor.

At the [2025 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2025) on June 14, Connor confronted his siblings, questioning their absence during [Anthony’s](anthony-iacovone) brutal beatdown. [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) pointed out that Connor didn’t ask for help, which only angered Connor. [Jenna](jenna-rosciszewski) cooled things down and rallied the trio, assuring her brothers that the [Family Business](family-business) would surely regain the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship if all three made it to the main event. However, none of the three qualified to the finals. After the main event, [Big British Billy](big-british-billy) took to the podium to celebrate the win of his accomplice, [Septilence](septilence). Suddenly, gunshots rang out in the air and Septilence tackled Billy to the floor. Septilence then escorted Billy to safety after a bloodied Billy raised his fist in the air, chanting “Fight! Fight! Fight!” in defiance to the apparent assassination attempt.

On July 4, [Big British Billy](billy-wager) accused [The Family Business](family-business) of orchestrating the attack and challenged any one of the trio to face him in Overwatch 2. Connor accepted the challenge and the match was set for July 11. At the event, Connor defeated Billy 9–1. Despite this, the siblings were informed on July 25 that they were ordered by court to take a polygraph test to prove their innocence. On September 19, the trio was interrogated by an anonymous MI6 agent. Ultimately, the lie detector determined the three were innocent, and they were free to go.

The drama between [The Family Business](family-business) and [Big British Billy](billy-wager) continued to boil from [Halloween Horror](halloween-horror-2025) until [Boomerang Fu: TURF WARS](boomerang-fu-2025) on December 27. There, the siblings defeated the [Big British Kingdom](big-british-kingdom) once and for all to end their nearly 2-year-long feud.

On January 8, 2026, Connor confronted [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar) following Ryan's shocking return to the GLCL at [Boomerang Fu: TURF WARS](boomerang-fu-2025). Connor took issue with Ryan “thinking he could just waltz back” after 3 years away. He defeated Ryan in a Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 Best-2-of-3 match.
