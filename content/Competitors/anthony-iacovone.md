# Anthony Iacovone

Anthony Iacovone is a GLCL competitor. He made his debut in the [2017 Mario Super Sluggers World Series](mario-super-sluggers-2017). A frequent main eventer, Anthony has competed for and defended the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship across four individual reigns. He is best known for his boastful and highly aggressive persona, most notably in his blood feuds against [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken), [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski), and [James Elliott](james-elliott). This aggression mirrors his success in the fighting game division, where he has dominated in games like Street Fighter, Mortal Kombat, and Gang Beasts.

# Competitive History

## Intercontinental Championship feuds (2017–2018)
Anthony debuted in the GLCL on May 10, 2017, replacing [Jenna Ward](jenna-ward) in the [Mario Super Sluggers World Series](mario-super-sluggers-2017). Despite losing, he made an instant splash in an electric match against [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski). He went on an extended hiatus due to a busy schedule, but he vowed to return in search of championship glory.

On January 13, 2018, Anthony entered the [WWE 2K18 Spice Cube Invitational](wwe-2018), teaming with [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) in the [Tag Team](tag-team) tournament. The two lost to [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski) & [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar). Anthony later lost to Connor in the first round of the singles tournament. This early encounter would precede a fateful rivalry.

On March 20, Anthony was eliminated from the [Mario Super Sluggers World Series](mario-super-sluggers-2018) in a heated match against [Bri Reilly](bri-reilly).

Anthony had a so-so summer that concluded with a Pac-Man tournament. On September 2, Iacovone lost to [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken) in the finals. This match was notable for being the first GLCL [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship match to feature two sons of immigrants.

Anthony was far from done with [Fintan](fintan-bracken). He challenged the [IC](intercontinental) Champion to a rematch in Super Mario Strikers on September 15. In a vicious and heart-stopping slugfest, Anthony defeated Fintan to win his first Intercontinental title. A final rubber match was scheduled for September 29 in Frogger. The two arch-rivals faced off one last time in a neck-and-neck battle. At the end of the night, Anthony’s hand was raised high to retain the title and win the feud. 

Anthony moved on to his next challenger: [Charlie Nunes](charlie-nunes). Charlie challenged the champion to a match in Slender: The Eight Pages on October 11. Charlie collected more pages than Anthony, beating the champ in an upset victory.

Anthony came up short in the finals of the [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2018) on November 24, despite an impressive performance.

## Second Intercontinental reign and The Hoodies (2019)

Anthony entered the Guitar Hero II Heavy Metal Tournament on March 15, 2019. He reencountered old rival [Bri Reilly](bri-reilly) in the finals, defeating her to take her Guitar Hero crown and claim the vacant GLCL [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship.

On May 18, Anthony retained his title in a squash match against [Jason Abbott](jason-abbott) in Mortal Kombat 11. Iacovone relinquished the title on June 23 as he could not defend it in the [Mario Super Sluggers World Series](mario-super-sluggers-2019).

At the [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2019) on November 30, Anthony formed an unlikely partnership with his former nemesis, [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken). Christening themselves [The Hoodies](hoodies), the two dominated the competition. The team tore through [Charlie Marthaler](charlie-marthaler) & [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar), [Matty B](matt-berlin) & [Dan Ruhe](dan-ruhe), and [James Elliott](james-elliott) & [Septilence](septilence) to win the vacant [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship. In the singles tournament, he was eliminated in the second round.

On December 21, [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) & [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski) challenged Anthony & [Fintan](fintan-bracken) for the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship. Despite an admirable performance, the challengers failed to reclaim the titles. Fintan gave the challengers props in a post-match interview, but asserted that [The Hoodies](hoodies) would be champions for a long time.

## Hiatus (2020–2021)
The [Intercontinental](intercontinental), [Tag Team](tag-team), and [Universal](universal) Championships were put on hiatus after the [Just Dance 4 Invitational](just-dance-2019), with the [Guitar Hero World Tour](guitar-hero-2020) scheduled for May 23, 2020. However, due to the COVID-19 pandemic, the event was cancelled and the hiatus was extended until further notice. [Fintan](fintan-bracken) & Anthony were scheduled to face [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) & [Spice Cube](devan-welch) in Rock Band at the Guitar Hero World Tour event.

## Return to chasing gold (2021–2023)
After almost two years of inactivity, the third annual [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2021) was announced on September 3, 2021. Anthony returned at the event on November 27. He and [Fintan](fintan-bracken) were eliminated in the first round of the [Tag Team](tag-team) tournament, but he clinched the victory in the finals of the singles tournament to win the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship for the third time. 

After the annual GLCL winter hiatus, Anthony returned to enter the [2022 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2022) on July 12. He was eliminated in the first round and thus lost the title. 

Anthony defeated the debuting [Jay Dickson](jay-dickson) in Round 1 of [Septilence Presents: The GLCL Carball Contender’s Series](carball-contenders-2022). Anthony made it to the semifinals before being eliminated by [Sam Webster](sam-webster) on November 5. He failed to make an impact at the [Ultimate Chicken Horse Smackdown](ultimate-chicken-horse-2022) in December.

Anthony was eliminated in the second round of the [2023 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2023) on July 7.

## Big British Kingdom (2024–2025)

Following a disappointing 2023, Anthony looked to recapture former glory by challenging [The Twins](twins) for the GLCL [Tag Team](tag-team) Championships at the [2024 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2024) on June 22. Despite [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) and [Connor's](connor-rosciszewski) pre-match beatdown removing [Charlie Nunes](charlie-nunes) from the equation, Anthony teamed with [Big Bad Billy](billy-wager) to shockingly win the titles. Furious that their plot backfired and cost them their titles, the Twins blindsided Billy with a ferocious attack in the parking lot after the event. 

On July 20, Anthony won his first [Universal](universal) Championship to become a [Triple Crown Champion](tags/triple-crown). Afterwards, he savagely attacked [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) while the latter was taking out the trash. Anthony warned that "\[he’s\] not gonna stop next time" after leaving Connor in a pool of his own blood.

On August 24, Anthony came in third place overall at the [2024 GLCL Olympics](olympics-2024). He earned the right to challenge for either the [Tag Team](tag-team) or [Universal](universal) titles, but he already had both.

On November 30, a 4-on-4 elimination match was scheduled to end the feud between [The Family Business](family-business) and the [Big British Kingdom](big-british-kingdom). Billy— now going by [Big British Billy](billy-wager) after [The Twins’](twins) numerous assaults left him with brain trauma and a new identity— recruited Anthony, [Septilence](septilence), and [Charlie Nunes](charlie-nunes) to fight alongside him. Unfortunately, due to severe weather, both [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) and honorary member [James](james-elliott) (who aligned with The Family Business due to a mutual disdain for Billy) pulled out of the event and the match was cancelled.

Dissatisfied with this non-conclusion and itching for a fight, [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) personally called out Anthony in February 2025. After trading barbs in an interview, Anthony broke into Connor’s home and shoved him into a washing machine on February 24. Connor laid out the official challenge for an [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship match in Worms W.M.D. On February 28, Connor defeated Anthony to retain the title. After the match, Anthony ambushed Connor and ran off with the title belt.

[Connor](connor-rosciszewski) emerged in March "asking nicely" for the title back. Anthony taunted Connor and refused to return the belt. After receiving a cryptic warning from Connor, Anthony found his fiancee, [Jade Rush](jade-rush), being held at knifepoint by a psychotic Connor. After a tense confrontation, Anthony managed to talk down Connor and hand over the belt. Furious, Anthony challenged Connor again, this time in Street Fighter V. On March 15, Connor lost the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship to Anthony. After the match, Anthony brutalized Connor with a steel chair and left him bloodied on the floor to definitively end the feud. 

In April, [Big British Billy](billy-wager) made an appearance on [BBB Radio](bbb-radio) to "congratulate" Anthony on his win, only to announce that he was removing Anthony from the [Big British Kingdom](big-british-kingdom) as he was now a threat to Billy and his Kingdom. Anthony was thus stripped of the GLCL [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship. Billy noted that while he had business to attend to, his right-hand man [Septilence](septilence) would face Anthony for the GLCL [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship in Mortal Kombat 1. 

On May 26, Anthony retained the title against [Septilence](septilence) in an exciting encounter. [The Kingdom](big-british-kingdom) vowed to return on June 14 in the [2025 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2025). In the main event, Septilence narrowly defeated [James Elliott](james-elliott), [Big British Billy](billy-wager), and Anthony to win his third [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship. After the event, Big British Billy took to the podium to celebrate the win of his accomplice. Suddenly, gunshots rang out in the air and Septilence tackled Billy to the floor. Septilence then escorted Billy to safety after a bloodied Billy raised his fist in the air, chanting “Fight! Fight! Fight!” in defiance to the apparent assassination attempt. 

## Various feuds (2025–2026)

After being demoted from the main event scene, Anthony began to feel bitter. On July 25, after running into [Tag Team](tag-team) Champion [Emily O’Neil](emily-oneil) (the partner of Anthony’s fiancee, [Jade Rush](jade-rush)), Anthony feigned congratulations before devolving into a cynical rant. Emily suggested that if Anthony had a problem with her, they could settle it in an Overcooked! 1v1. Jade questioned Anthony’s rude behavior towards her partner, but Anthony responded with irrational anger. Anthony defeated Emily and proceeded to excessively gloat. Jade then removed her engagement ring and shoved it into Anthony’s hands, calling off the wedding. Stricken with despair, Anthony simply gawked in disbelief.

In October, [Big S](sam-webster) bumped into Anthony and proposed an alliance. As Big S was looking to capture the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles to become the second-ever [Triple Champion](tags/triple-champion), Anthony saw an opportunity to gain revenge against [Jade](jade-rush) and [Emily](emily-oneil) by taking their titles. Convinced, Anthony agreed to the partnership and a Tag Team Championship match was set for December. 

On December 27 at [Boomerang Fu: TURF WARS](boomerang-fu-2025), Anthony suggested culpability for [Emily](emily-oneil) failing to appear due to slashed tires. A debuting [Belle Ayala](belle-ayala) volunteered to stand in, but Anthony & [Big S](sam-webster) defeated the duo to win the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles. Afterwards, Anthony defeated [Jade](jade-rush) in an impromptu singles match to "convince" her to reinstate their engagement, much to her chagrin. After the event, [James Elliott](james-elliott) ruthlessly ambushed Anthony with a crowbar, raving like a lunatic about feeling disrespected in the league.

On January 2, 2026, Anthony candidly admitted he was the only one in the GLCL who still respected [James](james-elliott) prior to their savage assault. The Outlaw then claimed James was past their “expiration date” and agreed to a Best-of-7 match in Call of Duty: Black Ops Cold War. On January 7, The Outlaw defeated James 4–3.
