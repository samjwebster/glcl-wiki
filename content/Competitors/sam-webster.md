Sam Webster is a [GLCL competitor](tags/competitor) known for his comedic promos and outlandish personas. He made his debut in late 2018 and would go on to become a [GLCL Triple Crown Champion](tags/triple-champion) in 2025. He is best known for his appearances as “Big S”, a bizarre alter ego that oozes charisma, arrogance, and “swag”. His unconventional presentation elevated him to numerous massive title wins in 2025. Sam’s dark horse status led him to surprisingly become the second-ever GLCL competitor to hold all three major titles at the same time, cementing himself in the history books as a main event legend.

# Competitive History
## Early Years (2018-2019)

Sam made his first appearance at [the Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2018) on November 24, 2018. He was eliminated in the second rounds of both the [Tag Team](tag-team) and [Intercontinental](intercontinental) tournaments. Four days later, he attempted to capture the [Universal Championship](universal) in an Air Hockey tournament, but was defeated by [Bri Reilly](bri-reilly) in the semifinals.

On April 18, 2019, Sam competed in a 2-on-1 Tag Team Championship handicap match against [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) & [Dan “The Man” Ruhe](dan-ruhe). Despite an incredible performance by Sam, the defending champions narrowly defeated him.

On June 8, Sam unsuccessfully challenged Universal Champion [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken) to a game of Checkers in San Antonio, Texas. This was the first time a GLCL title was defended outside of Cleveland, Ohio. Later that month on June 22, Sam defeated [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski) in a first round upset of the [Mario Super Sluggers World Series](mario-super-sluggers-2019). Sam would proceed to beat [Fintan](fintan-bracken) and [Joe Nappi](joe-nappi) in the quarterfinals and semifinals later that night. Two days later, Sam easily shut out [Charlie Nykiel](charlie-nykiel) 6 - 0 to win his first GLCL Intercontinental Championship. 17 days later, however, he would be forced to vacate the title after being informed he was going on a family vacation.

On August 12, Sam returned to reclaim his IC title in Tetris, out-scoring his opponents [Charlie Nykiel](charlie-nykiel), [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski), [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar), [Matty B](matt-berlin), [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall), [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken), and [Spice Cube](devan-welch). Sam was originally scheduled to defend the IC title against [Dan “The Man” Ruhe](dan-ruhe) on October 12, but both men pulled out of the event. Sam’s title was later stripped for inactivity. Despite his best efforts to regain gold at the [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2019) in November, Sam came up short. His final appearance in 2019 was a loss to [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski) and [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) in a Just Dance 4 3-way on December 27.

## GLCL Hiatus (2020-2021)
The Intercontinental, Tag Team, and Universal Championships were put on hiatus after Just Dance 4, with the [Guitar Hero World Tour](guitar-hero-2020) scheduled for May 23, 2020. However, due to the COVID-19 pandemic, the event was cancelled and the hiatus was extended until further notice. Sam was scheduled to compete in the Intercontinental Championship tournament at the Guitar Hero World Tour event.

## The Lake County Lollygaggers (2021-2022)
After almost two years of inactivity, the [third annual Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2021) was announced on September 3, 2021. Leading up to the event, Sam released a confident and definitionally 'sus' monologue declaring his participation. During the November 27 competition, after an accidental headbutt knocked out his partner, [Connor](connor-rosciszewski), the two were eliminated in the first round of the tag team tournament. He would then be eliminated in the second round of the singles tournament.

2022 was a rough year for Sam. A short-lived tag team stint with Matty B as Silicon Valley ended in defeat, followed by back-to-back Intercontinental tournament final losses in [Mario Kart 8](mario-kart-2022) and [Rocket League](carball-contenders-2022.md). After narrowly failing to reclaim the Intercontinental title numerous times, Sam found luck teaming with [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) and [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken) in Rocket League as [The Lake County Lollygaggers](lake-county-lollygaggers). The trio won Tag Team gold, Sam’s first in his career, on December 18. Unfortunately, they lost the titles only 12 days later in the [Ultimate Chicken Horse Smackdown](ultimate-chicken-horse-2022.md). Sam had a notably poor performance in the singles tournament, getting eliminated in the first round by [Big Bad Billy](billy-wager) and an extremely intoxicated [Fintan](fintan-bracken).

In early 2023, Sam fled to California, citing a need to “return to the drawing board”. 

## Rise of Big S (2023-present)

On May 18, 2023, in the lead-up to [Brother v Brother II](brother-vs-brother-ii), Sam made his first appearance as “Big S”, an unpredictable yet charismatic figure who more closely resembled a SnapChat filter than a real man. Showing support for his [“sugar brother”](tags/sugar-brother), [Connor](connor-rosciszewski), he flaunted his muscles and blue steel. He returned to the shadows, never to appear again for over 2 years.

On June 14, 2025, Sam returned to compete in his first match in 2 and a half years at the [Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2025). In his first match since December 2022, he defeated [James Elliott](james-elliott), [Emily O’Neil](emily-oneil), [Olivia Guenther](olivia-guenther), and [Professor Cube](devan-welch) to win his first [Universal Championship](universal) and become the [10th GLCL Triple Crown Champion](tags/triple-crown). 

On July 11, Sam defeated [Professor Cube](devan-welch) to retain the [Universal title](universal) in GamePigeon 8Ball. A week later, Sam reverted to his “Big S” persona to drop [an incendiary diss track on the entire GLCL](big-s-diss). He later revealed his plan to capture the [Tag Team](tag-team) and [Intercontinental](intercontinental) titles by the end of the year in order to become the “swaggiest” competitor in the GLCL. On August 1, the GLCL announced that Big S would compete in the [Valorant Free For All](valorant-2025), a three-way match against [Septilence](septilence) and [James Elliott](james-elliott) for the GLCL [Intercontinental Championship](intercontinental). On August 9, Big S defeated Septilence and James in an upset victory and achieving his third Intercontinental Champion status. After the match, Big S boasted that his mission was two-thirds from completion. Dubbing himself the “InterUniversal Champion”, he announced that he was on the lookout for a tag team partner that didn’t mind sharing the spotlight with him. 

On September 12, Big S ran into [The Twins](twins). Despite an attempt at casual conversation, [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) and [Connor Rosciszewski](rosciszewski) reacted with hostility and vowed to go after Big S’s Universal and Intercontinental titles. On October 20, Big S bumped into [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) and convinced The Outlaw to be his tag team partner, successfully appealing to Anthony’s animosity towards the GLCL [Tag Team](tag-team) Champions [Jade Rush](jade-rush) & [Emily O’Neil](emily-oneil).

At [Halloween Horror](halloween-horror-2025) on October 22, Big S defeated [Charlie Nunes](charlie-nunes) in Portal 2 to retain the GLCL Intercontinental Championship. 

On December 27 at [Boomerang Fu: TURF WARS](boomerang-fu-2025), [Anthony](anthony-iacovone) suggested culpability in [Emily](emily-oneil) failing to appear due to slashed tires. A debuting [Belle Ayala](belle-ayala) volunteered to stand in, but Anthony & Big S defeated her and [Jade](jade-rush) to win the [Tag Team titles](tag-team). In doing so, Big S completed his mission of capturing every GLCL title before the year’s end, becoming a [Triple Champion](tags/triple-champion) and vowing to transform the league to fit his vision of “swagginess”. [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar), the first man to hold all three GLCL titles at once, personally shook Big S’s hand after the match in a display of respect.

On January 9, 2026, [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) staked his claim to the [Intercontinental Championship](intercontinental). As the longest-reigning champion in the title’s history, Braden asserted that his claim to the title was absolute. Big S defeated Braden in Bloons TD 6 on February 8.

Big S’s [co-Tag Team Champion](tag-team), [Anthony](anthony-iacovone), respectfully declared that he was next in line for a shot at the [Intercontinental Championship](intercontinental). Big S obliged, and a match was set in Minigame Madness. On March 14, Big S defeated his partner to retain the title and broke [Braden Rosciszewski’s](braden-rosciszewski) 216-day record for longest GLCL Intercontinental Championship reign.

# Persona and Style

During the formative years of the GLCL, when the majority of the [competitor](tags/competitor) roster consisted of high school students, competitor personas typically functioned as exaggerated caricatures of their real-life personalities. During this period, Sam received limited on-screen representation in league trailers and promotional segments. 

Following the GLCL's return from its COVID-19 hiatus in 2021, Sam honed his creative direction. Rather than engaging with established characters, active rivalries, or the league's overarching storylines, he instead delivered absurd, detached appearances and anecdotes during promotional packages. His gimmick was formalized in 2023 after debuting his alter ego, Big S. Characterized by an aloof disregard for traditional league narratives, high-energy charisma, and unmistakable "swag", the persona quickly gained traction. 

Big S was elevated to main-event status in 2025, marked by feuds with high-profile competitors over the [Intercontinental Championship](intercontinental), including GLCL veterans [Septilence](septilence), [James Elliott](james-elliott), and [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone). The competitive and narrative success of these matches permanently solidified Sam's status as a premier main-event competitor.
