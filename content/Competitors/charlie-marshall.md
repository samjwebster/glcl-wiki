Charlie Marshall is a GLCL competitor. He made his debut in late 2018. A highly-decorated competitor, Charlie is a [Triple Crown Champion](tags/triple-crown). His unexpected rise in 2022 remains one of the most impressive breakout years in the GLCL. He is best known for his success in the Mario Kart Grand Prix, winning the [2022](mario-kart-2022) and [2024](mario-kart-2024) editions of the tournament. He is also widely considered the GLCL’s greatest of all time in sports games due to his dominance in games such as Madden, PGA Tour, and NBA 2K.

## Hiatus (2020–2021)
On December 21, Charlie and [Spice Cube](devan-welch) lost a [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship contendership match to the [Twins](twins), [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) & [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski) in Halo 3.

The [Intercontinental](intercontinental), [Tag Team](tag-team), and [Universal](universal) Championships were put on hiatus after the [Just Dance 4 Invitational](just-dance-2019), with the [Guitar Hero World Tour](guitar-hero-2020) scheduled for May 23, 2020. However, due to the COVID-19 pandemic, the event was cancelled and the hiatus was extended until further notice. Charlie & [Spice Cube](devan-welch) were scheduled to face [The Hoodies](hoodies) for the Tag Team Championship in Rock Band at the Guitar Hero World Tour event.

## Intercontinental Championship reigns (2021–Present)

After almost two years of inactivity, the third annual [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2021) was announced on September 3, 2021. Charlie made it to the finals of the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship tournament alongside longtime partner, [Spice Cube](devan-welch), but came up short against [James Elliott](james-elliott) & [Septilence](septilence). 

Marshall entered the [2022 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2022) on June 12, 2022, making it to the finals. He tied with [Septilence](septilence), leading to a Best-of-Three tiebreaker on every Rainbow Road map. Marshall defeated Septilence 2–1, winning the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) title for the first time in his career.

Charlie would go on to defend the title in the Madden NFL 22 Pro Bowl on July 9. He defeated his best friend and tag team partner, [Spice Cube](devan-welch), in the first round before successfully retaining the title against [Big Bad Billy](billy-wager) in the finals.

Marshall accepted a challenge from [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) in PGA Tour 2K21 on July 23. He defeated Braden to retain the title. He later lost the title after getting eliminated by [Matty B](matt-berlin) in Round 1 of [Septilence Presents: The GLCL Carball Contender’s Series](carball-contenders-2022) on September 10. His reign ended at 89 days.

Charlie returned on November 26 to defeat [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski) and [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar) in Jeopardy to win his second [Intercontinental](intercontinental) title. Although he expressed eagerness to defend the belt, [Big Bad Billy](billy-wager) implied he kidnapped Charlie on the December 16 episode of [Big Bashful Billy’s Beehive](big-bashful-billys-beehive). 

Charlie inexplicably returned in July 2023, at the [2023 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2023). Despite a strong showing, he came up short in the finals against [James Elliott](james-elliot).

Nearly one year later, Charlie returned at the [2024 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2024) to make good on the previous year’s failure, winning his third GLCL [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship. He successfully defended the title against recurring foe [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) in NBA 2K23 on July 20.

Charlie's [Intercontinental](intercontinental) title was vacated leading up to the [2024 GLCL Olympic Games](olympics-2024).
