James Elliott is a veteran GLCL competitor. They made their debut in the second-ever GLCL match in 2017. They quickly became an established main-event competitor, setting the foundation of the league with high-profile wins, feuds, and storylines against [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar), [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski), and [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski). After going off to college, James reestablished themselves as a dominant force in the midcard by holding the [Tag Team](tag-team) and [Universal](universal) titles, breaking the record for most days with the former and most reigns with the latter.

# Competitive History

## Initial struggles (2017)
James made their debut in Game 2 of the [Mario Party 8 Best-of-Six](mario-party-2017) series on January 28, 2017. Teaming with [Kourtney Syrus](kourtney-syrus), James came in fourth place. They continued to make appearances in the series, but did not make a significant impact until Game 5. On March 4, they helped cost [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) the match, allowing [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar) to become the inaugural [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Champion.

James’s next appearance was in the first annual [Mario Super Sluggers World Series](mario-super-sluggers-2017) on May 13. In the second round, James faced [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski). In an initially close game, Connor managed to defeat James 30–6. James entered the first annual [2017 Mario Kart Wii Grand Prix](mario-kart-2017) on June 15, but was eliminated from the tournament by [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar) and [Charlie Nykiel](charlie-nykiel) in the first round.


## Intercontinental Championship reigns (2017–2018)

> [!info]+
> ![[Images/competitors/james-elliott/elliott-elbow.webp]]
> > [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski) hitting James with an elbow drop ahead of the [Super Smash Bros. Brawl Classic](super-smash-bros-2017)

[Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski) defeated [Dan Ruhe](dan-ruhe) to win the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship on August 19. He immediately issued a challenge to James, [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar), and [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) in Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare. James accepted the challenge, and on September 16, they swept the three competitors to win the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship in dominant fashion. 

After claiming the title, James adopted an arrogant, aggressive persona. A Twitter poll was launched soon after the Modern Warfare four-way match, allowing fans to determine the next number-one contender for the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) title. [Ryan](ryan-yanchar) edged out [Septilence](septilence), [Dan Ruhe](dan-ruhe), and [Charlie Nykiel](charlie-nykiel) to earn a title shot. James and Ryan agreed to battle in EA Sports UFC 2 on September 28, where James secured a decisive victory in five rounds.

Since he placed second in the Twitter poll, [Septilence](septilence) was next in line to compete for James’s title. The two faced off in Wheel of Fortune on October 13, with James again coming out on top. Afterward, James and [Ryan](ryan-yanchar) reluctantly teamed up to face [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) & [Septilence](septilence) in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 3. Connor & Mat won the match to secure the inaugural [Tag Team](tag-team)Championships.

On October 21, [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) challenged James to a title match in Kinect Sports. Although the opening round in soccer ended in a draw, Braden took the lead in bowling. Braden could not maintain momentum, however, as James took control and won the following four rounds. Braden immediately attributed his loss to the Kinect’s poor motion controls. 

[Bri Reilly](bri-reilly) issued a challenge to James in Guitar Hero III: Legends of Rock. On November 22, the two competed in a nearly four-hour match. Bri eventually emerged victorious, silencing the champion and capturing the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) title.

James continued a competitive decline, losing in the second round of the [Super Smash Bros. Brawl Invitational](super-smash-bros-2017) on December 30. On January 13, James met [Connor Roscizewski](connor-rosciszewski) in the finals of the [WWE 2K18 Spice Cube Invitational](wwe-2018). In a brutal Last Man Standing match, Connor prevailed over James. This loss incited a deep resentment from James toward Connor.

James soon challenged [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) to a rematch in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2. Seeing this as a chance at redemption for his previous loss in September 2017, Connor accepted. [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar) and [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) both insisted that they deserved to be in the match as well, leading to the creation of a four-way match on February 16. Despite a much stronger showing by Ryan, Connor, and Braden, the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship was reclaimed by James across seven rounds.

James went on to defeat the debuting [Emily O’Neil](emily-oneil) and [Hannah Syrus](hannah-syrus) in the [Mario Super Sluggers World Series](mario-super-sluggers-2018). In the semifinals on April 7, however, [Septilence](septilence) defeated James, eliminating them from the tournament and vacating the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship.

On May 19, James challenged [Septilence](septilence) for the vacant [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship in Gang Beasts, alongside longtime rivals [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) and [Ryan](ryan-yanchar). The four competitors battled for 25 consecutive rounds. Eventually, James, Ryan, and Mat reached a three-way, seven-win tie. In the final round, James and Mat fought on top of a hanging shipping container; James outlasted Mat to win their third [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship. On May 27, James defeated then-champion Connor, as well as Ryan, [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski), [Braden Roscizewski](braden-rosciszewski), and [Harry Yanchar](harry-yanchar) to win the [Universal](universal) Championship for the first time. 

[Connor](connor-rosciszewski) challenged James to a grudge match in Halo: Reach on June 1, claiming that James could not defeat him one-on-one. In the encounter, Connor managed to keep an edge on the champion, but James persistently closed the scoring gap. The game ended in a tie, leaving no clear winner. While a tie traditionally dictates that the champion retains, James chose to vacate the title out of a newfound respect for their opponent. 

### Part-Time Status (2018–2019)
James won the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship for a fourth time on July 16 in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 3. In the third and final four-way encounter between themselves, [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar), [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski), and [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski), James clean swept their opponents again to secure their legacy as a Call of Duty specialist. They defended the title on August 6 against Ryan and Braden in Minecraft. Although they successfully defended the championship, James began to show apathy toward the belt. They were stripped of the title on August 15 after requesting to vacate it. 

James returned at the [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2018) on November 24, promising to enact vengeance on their enemies. They instead were eliminated in the first round of the [tag team](tag-team) tournament (teaming with [Emily O’Neil](emily-oneil)) and the second round of the singles tournament.

James next appeared at the [Just Dance 3 Invitational](just-dance-2018) on December 22. They defeated the likes of [Elise Provident](elise-provident), [Kourtney Syrus](kourtney-syrus), and [Alyssa Desotell](alyssa-desotell) to advance to the finals. In the main event, James faced their arch-rival, [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski). Connor defeated James to retain the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship, but James goaded the champion afterward. Connor refused to fall for their old rival’s tricks, instead telling James they would have to earn a shot at the title in the [WWE 2K19 Spice Cube Invitational](wwe-2019). At that event, however, James failed to appear. On December 28, James faced [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar) in the first round of an Air Hockey tournament for the [Universal](universal) title in a losing effort. James left for college again soon after.

James returned on July 12, 2019, at the [Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2019). They lost a [Universal](universal) Championship match against [Spice Cube](devan-welch), [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski), and [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar), a [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship match against [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) & [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski), and were eliminated in the first round of the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship tournament.

On November 30, James appeared at the [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2019). Boasting that the hatred of their enemies only fueled their desire to win, James proceeded to win the [Universal](universal) Championship after defeating [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken) and [Sam Webster](sam-webster) in a Guess Who? tournament. James came up short in the finals of the [Tag Team](tag-team) title tournament, teaming with occasional partner [Septilence](septilence), and was eliminated in the first round of the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship tournament. James shrugged off their losses and asserted that the [Universal](universal) Championship was their only priority. 

## Hiatus (2020–2021)

On December 27, James defended the [Universal](universal) Championship at the [Just Dance 4 Invitational](just-dance-2019) against [Emily O’Neil](emily-oneil), retaining the title in a short game of Rebound. They later fought their way to the semifinals of the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) title tournament, falling to longtime rival [Bri Reilly](bri-reilly). James returned to college shortly afterward, taking the [Universal](universal) Championship with them. 

The [Intercontinental](intercontinental), [Tag Team](tag-team), and [Universal](universal) Championships were put on hiatus after Just Dance 4. James was scheduled to defend the title at the [Guitar Hero World Tour](guitar-hero-2020) tournament on May 23, 2020, but the COVID-19 pandemic led to the cancellation of the event and an indefinite extension of the GLCL hiatus. With no foreseeable end to the pandemic, the [Universal](universal) Championship was officially vacated on May 23, 2020.

## Record-breaking Tag Team Reign and The Hive (2021–2023)

After almost two years of inactivity, the third annual [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2021) was announced on September 3, 2021. James returned at the event on November 27 and won both the [Universal](universal) and, with [Septilence](septilence), [Tag Team](tag-team) Championships. James was scheduled to defend their titles at the [Just Dance Greatest Hits Invitational](just-dance-2021) in December. However, due to mounting COVID concerns, the tournament was canceled and the GLCL winter hiatus began early.

The summer season began with the [2022 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2022). James successfully retained the [Universal](universal) title against Braden Rosciszewski, Connor Rosciszewski, and [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Champion Anthony Iacovone. They also successfully defended the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles against [Sam Webster](sam-webster) & [Matty B](matt-berlin) of [Silicon Valley](silicon-valley). 

James lost the [Universal](universal) Championship to their own tag team partner, [Septilence](septilence), in a game of Connect 4 on July 9, 2022. This ended their record-shattering 224-day reign. James was slated to face [Sam Webster](sam-webster) in the first round of the [Septilence Presents: The GLCL Carball Contender’s Series](carball-contenders-2022). On September 30, Sam defeated James to eliminate them. They subsequently lost the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championships to [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken) & [Sam Webster](sam-webster) of the [Lake County Lollygaggers](lake-county-lollygaggers) on December 18.

James was later recruited into [Big Bad Billy’s](billy-wager) cult, [The Hive](hive). After forming the [Friends in Fear](friends-in-fear) tag team with fellow Hive cultist [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski), James reclaimed the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championships in Fall Guys against three other teams on March 11, 2023.

James won the [2023 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2023) on July 7, taking home the trophy and earning the right to face [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Champion [Braden Rosciszewski](braden-rosciszewski) at a later date. Earlier in the night, the [Friends in Fear](friends-in-fear), successfully retained the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship against [Olivia Guenther](olivia-guenther) and [Shannon Wright](shannon-wright). After Braden defeated Jenna in the main event, James and [The Hive](hive) taunted Braden over the looming threat to come.

James challenged [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) for the GLCL [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship on October 21, 2023, exactly six years after they previously faced off for the title in Kinect Sports. This time, however, Braden defeated James in Rocket League to retain the title.

On November 23, [Big Bad Billy](billy-wager) stood in for James as the [Friends in Fear](friends-in-fear) defended the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship against the newly reunited [Twins](twins), [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) and [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski), in Nintendo Switch Sports. The Friends in Fear failed to secure the victory, and the Twins became two-time GLCL Tag Team Champions. Afterward, Billy blamed the loss on James's failure to appear and officially ousted them from [The Hive](hive).

### Lone Wolf (2024–Present)

Over a year later, James came out of hiding to broker a deal with [The Family Business](family-business). Despite their past enmity with the three, their mutual disdain for now [Big British Billy](billy-wager) was enough for James to agree to a partnership. The quartet was set to battle Big British Billy's [Big British Kingdom](big-british-kingdom) (Billy, [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone), [Septilence](septilence), and [Charlie Nunes](charlie-nunes)) in Super Smash Bros. Ultimate. However, on November 30, 2024, inclement weather forced the GLCL to cancel the match.

James returned at the [2025 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2025) on June 14, 2025. Despite advancing to the finals, they narrowly lost to [Septilence](septilence) after a last-second red shell. Later, they staked their claim to Septilence’s [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship and blasted their former friend and partner’s new allegiance to [Big British Billy](billy-wager). On July 11, Septilence barely escaped James with the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) title after a thrilling 1v1 match in Overwatch 2. Later that month, [Sam Webster](sam-webster)’s alter-ego, Big S, dropped a [diss track](big-s-diss) calling out both James and Septilence. 

The GLCL announced the three would face off in a [Valorant Free For All](valorant-2025) for the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) title. On August 9, 2025, [Big S](sam-webster) defeated [Septilence](septilence) and James in a surprising upset. On October 10, James approached Septilence with a proposition to settle the score: a one-on-one match for the right to challenge Big S. At [Halloween Horror](halloween-horror-2025) on October 22, Septilence outlasted James in a Five Nights at Freddy’s Survival Match to earn the championship opportunity. 

Following [Boomerang Fu: TURF WARS](boomerang-fu-2025) on December 27, 2025, James blindsided [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) with a crowbar attack, citing a feeling of disrespect from Anthony and the rest of the GLCL. On January 2, 2026, James asserted that they were going to make an example out of Anthony. The Outlaw then claimed James was past their "expiration date" and agreed to a match in Call of Duty: Black Ops Cold War. On January 7, 2026, in a gripping duel, Anthony narrowly defeated James 4–3. Unmoved, James continued their aggressive campaign, and a rematch was subsequently scheduled in Clone Hero.
