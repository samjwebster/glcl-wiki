Braden Rosciszewski is a founding member of the GLCL. He made his debut in the first-ever GLCL competition. A highly-decorated competitor, Braden holds the record for the longest consecutive [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship reign in addition to five [Tag Team](tag-team) and three [Universal](universal) titles, making him a [Triple Crown Champion](tags/triple-crown). His feuds against rivals such as [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski), [Big Bad Billy](billy-wager), and [James Elliott](james-elliott) have cemented his status as a main-event star. 

# Chronological History

## Debut and early pursuits (2017)

On January 14, 2017, Braden debuted in Game 1 of the [Mario Party 8 Best-of-Six](mario-party-2017) series--the first match in the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship’s history. Braden lost the first three games to [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar), but won Game 4 on February 24 to turn the tides of the series. If Braden could tie the series by winning Games 5 and 6, then the winner of the inaugural Intercontinental Championship would have been decided in a select number of minigames. However, Braden’s brother, [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski), won Game 5 on March 4 to cost Braden the series and the Intercontinental title.

Despite the setback, Braden entered the first annual [Mario Super Sluggers World Series](mario-super-sluggers-2017) and defeated a debuting [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) in an intense match to advance to the quarterfinals on May 10. Three days later, he beat the debuting [Alexis Cardinale](alexis-cardinale) to progress to the semifinals. On June 3, Braden again lost to [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) and was eliminated from the tournament.

Braden entered the first annual [2017 Mario Kart Wii Grand Prix](mario-kart-2017) on June 15, but was eliminated in the first round.

On September 16, Braden competed in a 4-way match against [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Champion [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski), [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar), and [James Elliott](james-elliott) in Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare. Braden consistently came in last place in each match, ultimately failing to capture the title.

On October 21, Braden challenged the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Champion, [James Elliott](james-elliott), in Kinect Sports. After a few controversial rounds, James defeated the challenger. Braden angrily protested the loss, blaming the motion controls of the Kinect and demanding a rematch.

## Championship reigns (2017–2019)

Braden teamed up with [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar) to challenge [Tag Team](tag-team) Champions [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski) & [Mat Corfee](septilence) in Madden NFL 17, on December 22. Braden won his first title after an agonizingly long match.

Braden then appeared in the [Super Smash Bros. Brawl Classic](super-smash-bros-2017) on December 30, losing to [Alex Ruhe](alex-ruhe) in the first round.

On January 5, Braden defended the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles with [Ryan](ryan-yanchar) in a losing effort against his father, [Scott](scott-rosciszewski), and his sister, [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski). Braden and Ryan disbanded their alliance as a result.

On January 13, Braden entered the [WWE 2K18 Spice Cube Invitational](wwe-2018). In the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship tournament, he teamed with [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone). The two lost to Braden’s rival, [Connor](connor-rosciszewski), and his former tag team partner, [Ryan](ryan-yanchar). In the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship tournament, Braden beat [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken) to advance to the second round. Nevertheless, Connor again defeated his brother and went on to win the tournament.

The next day, Braden rebounded with a win over [Ryan](ryan-yanchar), [Connor](connor-rosciszewski), and [James](james-elliott) in Scrabble to secure his first [Universal](universal) Championship.

Braden defeated [Spice Cube](devan-welch) in a contendership match for the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship to earn the rematch he demanded in October 2017. On February 16, he got another crack at the IC title in the second 4-way match between himself, [Connor](connor-rosciszewski), [James](james-elliott), and [Ryan](ryan-yanchar). This time, the four faced off in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2. To Braden’s dismay, James once again came out on top. That same night, Braden lost the [Universal](universal) Championship to Connor in a Cards Against Humanity 5-way also involving James, Ryan, and [Septilence](septilence). 

In the second annual [Mario Super Sluggers World Series](mario-super-sluggers-2018), Braden was scheduled to face [Septilence](septilence), who Braden promised to dominate. On March 10, Braden was decisively eliminated in the first round. 

On March 16, Braden reclaimed the [Universal](universal) Championship from [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) in a Candy Land 3-way also involving [Scott Rosciszewski](scott-rosciszewski). Braden became the first-ever two-time Universal Champion as a result. He dropped the title back to Connor in B.S. on March 30, in a match also involving [Ryan](ryan-yanchar) and [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski).

At the [Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2018) on June 15, Braden participated in a Monopoly Gamer: Mario Kart 4-way against [Ryan](ryan-yanchar), [James](james-elliott), and [Spice Cube](devan-welch) for the [Universal](universal) Championship. Braden would fail to recapture the Universal title. Later in the night, he was eliminated in the second round of the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship tournament.

Braden competed in the third Call of Duty 4-way involving [Connor](connor-rosciszewski), [James](james-elliott), and [Ryan](ryan-yanchar) on July 16, this time in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 3. In the final match of the trilogy, Braden put up the best fight of the series. However, he ultimately fell to James again.

On August 6, Braden rage quit a Minecraft Wipeout 3-way against [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Champion [James](james-elliott) and [Ryan](ryan-yanchar).

After a six-month drought, Braden finally won another title by teaming up with [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken) to beat [Alexis Cardinale](alexis-cardinale) & [Hannah Syrus](hannah-syrus) in New Super Mario Bros. Wii for the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship on October 13. He and Fintan defended the titles on November 1 against [Spice Cube](devan-welch) and a returning [Connor Rosciszewski](connor-rosciszewski). 

On November 24, at the [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2018), Braden & [Fintan](fintan-bracken) fought their way through the [Tag Team](tag-team) tournament. In the finals, the Tag Team Champions defeated [Spice Cube](devan-welch) & [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) in a brutal match to retain the titles and become the first Tag Team champs to walk in and out of a tournament with the titles. Braden did not fare as well in the singles tournament, which he was eliminated from in the first round. 

On January 12, 2019, Braden defended the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles with [Fintan](fintan-bracken) at the [WWE 2K19 Spice Cube Invitational](wwe-2019). In a shocking upset, the champions lost to [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) & [Charlie Nunes](charlie-nunes). As a result, Braden & Fintan were eliminated and their titles were vacated. In the singles tournament, Braden defeated [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) and [Dan Ruhe](dan-ruhe) to advance to the finals. In the finals, Braden faced his brother and arch-nemesis, [Connor](connor-rosciszewski), in an Iron Man match. Connor beat Braden to retain the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) title, but Braden immediately demanded another shot at the title. Incensed, Connor granted Braden another chance in a No-Disqualification match. Connor finally defeated his elder brother to retain the Intercontinental Championship. Afterwards, Connor was surprised by Braden’s sportsmanlike acceptance of defeat. Connor agreed to give Braden one last opportunity to face him on February 17.

On February 2, the brothers teamed up as [The Twins](twins) to face [Tag Team](tag-team) Champions [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) & [Dan Ruhe](dan-ruhe) in the last part of a gauntlet match. After Braden cost the duo the game, and consequently the Tag Team titles, [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) angrily got in his brother’s face. The two traded verbal jabs before the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Champion raised the belt into the air, setting the stage for their final encounter. 

On February 17, Braden and [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) faced off in Peggle. After a long and grueling battle, Connor emerged victorious. Braden would go on a hiatus for a few months afterwards.

On April 18, Braden shockingly turned on his [tag team](tag-team) partner, [Fintan](fintan-bracken), and sided with none other than [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) to challenge [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) for the Tag Team Championship in a 2-on-1 handicap match in NCAA Football 14. Two days later, he battled Charlie in Cars: The Video Game alone, and lost. He finally defeated Charlie in Madden NFL 19 to win the Tag Team titles for [The Twins](twins).

Braden & [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) defended the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championship against [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) & [Spice Cube](devan-welch) in Cornhole on June 2. 

At the third annual [Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2019) on July 12, Braden defeated [Universal](universal) Champion [Spice Cube](devan-welch) in a Monopoly Gamer: Mario Kart 4-way also involving [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar) and [James Elliott](james-elliott) to win the Universal title a third time. Alongside [Connor](connor-rosciszewski), he retained the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles against James & Ryan. In the [IC](intercontinental) tournament, Braden lost in the second round.

On August 7, Braden lost the [Universal](universal) title after getting eliminated by [Matty B](matt-berlin) in a Chess tournament.

Three days later, he unsuccessfully challenged [Charlie Nykiel](charlie-nykiel) for the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship in a Tetris 8-pack match also involving [Sam Webster](sam-webster), [Spice Cube](devan-welch), [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken), [Ryan Yanchar](ryan-yanchar), [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall), and [Matty B](matt-berlin).

On August 12, Braden & [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) retained the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles against [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) & [Spice Cube](devan-welch) in Wii Sports. Exactly two months later, [The Twins](twins) faced Marshall & Spice in Wii Party. The match ended in a tie, triggering the challengers to promise vengeance at the upcoming [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2019).

At the second annual [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2019) on November 30, [Marshall](charlie-marshall) & [Spice](devan-welch) unceremoniously eliminated [The Twins](twins) in the first round to a chorus of cheers. Braden & [Connor's](connor-rosciszewski) record-breaking reign ended at 224 days. In the singles tournament, Braden defeated [James Elliott](james-elliott) and [Charlie Marthaler](charlie-marthaler) to advance to the second round alongside [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone). Despite a strong performance, Braden was eliminated in the semifinals.

On December 21, Braden & [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) defeated [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) & [Spice Cube](devan-welch) in Halo 3 for a [Tag Team](tag-team) title opportunity against the winners of the [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2019), [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) & [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken). Despite an admirable performance, the challengers failed to reclaim the championship. After the match, Connor viciously turned on Braden to instantly disband the team. Connor later elaborated on his actions, claiming his brother was dead weight. He cited his success in the Gang Beasts singles tournament as proof that he was better off working alone. 

On December 27, Braden was eliminated in the first round of the [Just Dance 4 Invitational](just-dance-2019).

## Hiatus (2020–2021)

The [Intercontinental](intercontinental), [Tag Team](tag-team), and [Universal](universal) Championships were put on hiatus after the [Just Dance 4 Invitational](just-dance-4), with the [Guitar Hero World Tour](guitar-hero-2020) scheduled for May 23, 2020. However, due to the COVID-19 pandemic, the event was cancelled and the hiatus was extended until further notice. Braden was scheduled to face Universal Champion [James Elliott](james-elliott) and [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) in a 3-way match at the Guitar Hero World Tour event. 

After almost two years of inactivity, the third annual [Gang Beasts World Cup](gang-beasts-2021) was announced on September 3, 2021. Braden returned at the event on November 27, but came up short in both tag team and singles action.

## Feud with Big Bad Billy, the Lake County Lollygaggers, and Brother vs. Brother II (2022–2023)
Braden failed to win [Universal](universal) or [Intercontinental](intercontinental) gold at the [2022 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2022) on June 12. Following the tournament, [Big Bad Billy](billy-wager) released a scathing promo lambasting Braden. Accusing his former mentor of trying to piggyback off of Billy’s success, Billy promised to dismantle Braden at the Madden 22 Pro Bowl. On July 7, Braden shockingly lost to Billy just as his former protege predicted, albeit at the hands of a game glitch.

Braden challenged [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) for the latter’s [IC](intercontinental) title in PGA Tour 2K21 on July 23. Despite mounting several comebacks, early trap pitfalls held Braden back from being able to defeat the champion.

On September 17, Braden entered [Septilence Presents: The GLCL Carball Contender’s Series](carball-contenders-2022) and defeated his brother [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) 1-on-1 for the first time ever. Afterwards, Connor began peddling conspiracy theories on social media accusing Braden of cheating, even starting the hashtag ``#BradenFraud''. After learning that his next opponent would be [Billy](billy-wager), the demon he failed to slay in Madden, Braden overzealously requested his next match be pushed up to October 7. His audacity proved to be his undoing, and Billy once again defeated his former mentor to eliminate Braden from the tournament.

In November, [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) staged an ``#OccupyGLCL'' protest in Columbus, Ohio. He led a rally bemoaning the GLCL’s "failure" to stop Braden’s purported fraud, and claimed "the people" wanted to see him as GLCL [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Champion. 

On December 22, Braden captured the [Tag Team](tag-team) Championships as part of the [Lake County Lollygaggers](lake-county-lollygaggers), alongside [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken) and [Sam Webster](sam-webster). However, on December 30, at the [Ultimate Chicken Horse Smackdown](ultimate-chicken-horse-2022), [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) won the [IC](intercontinental) title for a sixth time and secured Braden’s Tag Team titles (alongside [Septilence](septilence)) to add insult to injury. Following the main event, the two exchanged heated remarks.

[Connor](connor-rosciszewski) ruthlessly insulted Braden and denied him a championship match on February 17, 2023. On April 14, Braden dressed Connor down, calling him “pathetic”, “nothing”, and accusing Connor of dodging him. A week later, Connor mused about this possibly being his “last chance to prove” that he was better than Braden and accepted Braden’s challenge. An [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship match was set for May 27 in EA Sports UFC 2. At [Brother vs. Brother II](brother-vs-brother-ii), Braden silenced Connor with a first-round knockout to finally win his first GLCL Intercontinental Championship.

Braden made an address to the GLCL on June 17, asserting that his business with [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) was firmly in the past. He was interrupted by [Fintan Bracken](fintan-bracken), who insulted Braden and challenged him to a match for the [IC](intercontinental) title at the [Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2023). Fintan later pulled out of the event and was replaced by [Jenna Rosciszewski](jenna-rosciszewski). On July 7, Braden defeated Jenna in an acclaimed match. After the main event, [James Elliott](james-elliott) (who earned an IC title opportunity by winning the tournament earlier that night), Jenna, and [Big Bad Billy](billy-wager) stood tall as [The Hive](hive), cementing themselves as Braden’s rivals.

On October 21, Braden defended the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship against [James](james-elliott) exactly six years after they last faced off for the title in Kinect Sports. This time around, Braden had James’ number in Rocket League as he successfully vanquished the second [Friend in Fear](friend-in-fear), taking out both of [Big Bad Billy’s](billy-wager) cultists. Braden personally selected Billy as his next challenger, wanting to cut off the head of the snake to finally destroy the monster he created.

On November 23, Braden shockingly reunited with [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) to reform [The Twins](twins) and defeat [Jenna](jenna-rosciszewski) & [Big Bad Billy](billy-wager) (who stood in for James) for the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles. Having defeated both of Billy’s cultists and taken their titles, Braden looked to carry that momentum into Boomerang Fu for the final battle with his nemesis.

On December 30, Braden attempted to become the second-ever competitor to hold all three titles at once when he faced [Universal](universal) Champion [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall), [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone), and the eventual winner [Emma McLaughlin](emma-mclaughlin) in Dumb Ways to Die: The Card Game. Retaining the [Tag Team](tag-team) titles with [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) against [Olivia Guenther](olivia-guenther) & [Shannon Wright](shannon-wright), Braden turned his attention to the final battle against [Big Bad Billy](billy-wager). Despite a valiant performance, Braden simply could not defeat his powerful foe. Even so, Braden left behind an impressive record for longest individual reign with the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship at 216 days. He stormed off, dropping the title on the floor and spitting in the face of the dastardly villain.

## The Family Business (2024–Present)

[The Twins](twins) shockingly aligned themselves with [Big Bad Billy](billy-wager) on March 31, 2024 before immediately betraying him and revealing their true allegiance to his “Wisewoman”, [Jenna](jenna-rosciszewski). The Twins beat Billy down before Jenna defeated him in Just Dance Greatest Hits to claim his [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship. The three siblings henceforth dubbed themselves [The Family Business](family-business) and vowed to enact violence on everyone in their path.

Braden was eliminated in the first round of the [2024 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2024) on June 22. [Anthony Iacovone](anthony-iacovone) & [Charlie Nunes](charlie-nunes) were scheduled to challenge Braden & [Connor](connor-rosciszewski) for their GLCL [Tag Team](tag-team) Championships, but the [Twins](twins) revealed that they brutally assaulted Charlie before the event. Without a partner and with his back to the wall, Anthony made an alliance with [Big Bad Billy](billy-wager). The duo shockingly defeated Braden & Connor. Furious that their plot backfired and cost them their titles, the Twins blindsided Billy again with a ferocious attack in the parking lot after the event. 

On July 20, Braden unsuccessfully challenged [Charlie Marshall](charlie-marshall) for the GLCL [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship in NBA 2K23.

On November 30, a 4-on-4 elimination match was scheduled to end the feud between [The Family Business](family-buseinss) and [Billy](billy-wager). Billy--now going by Big British Billy after [The Twins’](twins) numerous assaults left him with brain trauma and a new identity--recruited [Anthony](anthony-iacovone), [Septilence](septilence), and [Charlie Nunes](charlie-nunes) to fight alongside him as the [Big British Kingdom](big-british-kingdom). Unfortunately, due to severe weather, both [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) and honorary member [James](james-elliott) (who aligned with The Family Business due to a mutual disdain for Billy) pulled out of the event and the match was cancelled.

At the [2025 Mario Kart Grand Prix](mario-kart-2025) on June 14, Connor confronted his siblings, questioning their absence during [Anthony’s](anthony-iacovone) brutal beatdown. [Braden](braden-rosciszewski) pointed out that Connor didn’t ask for help, which only angered Connor. [Jenna](jenna-rosciszewski) cooled things down and rallied the trio, assuring her brothers that the [Family Business](family-business) would surely regain the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship if all three made it to the main event. However, none of the three qualified to the finals. After the main event, [Big British Billy](big-british-billy) took to the podium to celebrate the win of his accomplice, [Septilence](septilence). Suddenly, gunshots rang out in the air and Septilence tackled Billy to the floor. Septilence then escorted Billy to safety after a bloodied Billy raised his fist in the air, chanting “Fight! Fight! Fight!” in defiance to the apparent assassination attempt.

On July 4, [Big British Billy](billy-wager) accused [The Family Business](family-business) of orchestrating the attack and challenged any one of the trio to face him in Overwatch 2. Connor accepted the challenge and the match was set for July 11. At the event, Connor defeated Billy 9–1. Despite this, the siblings were informed on July 25 that they were ordered by court to take a polygraph test to prove their innocence. On September 19, the trio was interrogated by an anonymous MI6 agent. Ultimately, the lie detector determined the three were innocent, and they were free to go.

At [Boomerang Fu: TURF WARS](boomerang-fu-2025) on December 27, Braden and his siblings defeated the [Big British Kingdom](big-british-kingdom) once and for all to end their nearly 2-year-long feud.

On January 9, 2026, Braden staked his claim to the [Intercontinental](intercontinental) Championship. As the longest-reigning champion in the title’s history, Braden asserted that his claim to the title was absolute. [Big S](sam-webster) would go on to defeat Braden in Bloons TD 6 on February 8.
